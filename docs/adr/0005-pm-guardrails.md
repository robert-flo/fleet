# PM guardrails: template, delivery contract, quota, escalation

- Every PM is created by the CEO from one template (skill create-pm, still to write): same prompt, skills and limits; only the Área changes.
- The `ready-for-agent` label on a ticket is the only signal that Etapa 2 may execute it, and a PM applies it only when the ticket has verifiable acceptance criteria.
- PMs have no routines of their own and only work when Roberto talks to them; the CEO's weekly Auditoría de flota is the fleet's only routine.
- When a decision affects another PM or conflicts with the fleet vision, the PM stops and escalates to its Gerente regional, or to the CEO if none exists, instead of deciding alone.
